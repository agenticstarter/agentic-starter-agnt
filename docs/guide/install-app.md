# Install the app on your phone

Agentic Starter works as an app on your phone: full screen, tabs at the bottom, one tap from your home screen.
It is the website itself, so there is nothing to update and no app store. Your wallet still asks before every
signature.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open [agenticstarter.xyz](https://agenticstarter.xyz) in **Safari**. Inside Telegram, X or a wallet app, open the
   page in Safari first: other iPhone browsers may not add web apps.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow) in Safari's bar. If you don't see it, tap **⋯** first.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open Agentic Starter from your home screen.

## Android

### Chrome

1. Open [agenticstarter.xyz](https://agenticstarter.xyz) in **Chrome**. If the link opened inside Telegram, X or a
   wallet app, tap **⋮** at the top right and choose **Open in Chrome** first: apps cannot be installed from there.
2. Tap **⋮** at the top right of Chrome.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.
4. Open Agentic Starter from your home screen or your app list.

When Chrome shows an **Install** button on the page or in the address bar, tapping it does the same.

### Samsung Internet

1. Tap **☰** at the bottom.
2. Tap **Add page to**, then **Home screen**.

## In the app

- **Language:** tap your picture at the top left to open the menu, then **Settings → Language** and pick from the
  list.
- **App lock:** **Settings → App lock** asks for Face ID, a fingerprint, the device passcode or your own password
  before the app opens. The lock stays on your phone.
- **Avatar:** tap your picture in the menu to choose a photo or a colour. It stays on your phone; nothing is
  uploaded.

## Questions

**I don't see Install app.** Update Chrome from the Play Store and reload the page. If the app is installed
already, the same menu shows **Open app** instead.

**Is it safe?** It is the same site as agenticstarter.xyz and cannot reach your wallet's keys: every transaction
still opens your wallet for approval. Install only from agenticstarter.xyz. The team never sends app files or links
in private messages.

**How do I remove it?** Like any app: press and hold the icon, then **Remove** or **Uninstall**.
