## Check before you sign

[Site integrity](https://agenticstarter.xyz/integrity) lists every official contract and Safe address, every
parameter change, the official links and the current build of each site. A watcher compares the live sites with the
published builds; if a site changes outside a planned deploy, it goes into maintenance until the team checks it.
Known fake domains are listed there too.

Before you approve a transaction:

1. The site address is `agenticstarter.xyz` (or a subdomain) and the padlock is there.
2. The contract your wallet shows matches Site integrity.
3. You never approve "unlimited" spending: our sites always ask for the exact amount.

## Token risks

A token launched here can still go to zero. Tokens on the curve have no owner, no mint and no tax, and the liquidity
is burned at graduation, but a creator can sell their own tokens like anyone else. Read [Risks](/risks).

## Report a problem

- Security bug: [agenticstarter.xyz/security](https://agenticstarter.xyz/security) or security@agenticstarter.xyz.
  Please give the team time to fix it before you share it publicly. Confirmed bugs earn bounty points.
- Scam or fake admin in Telegram: reply to the message with `/report`.
- Something is down: see [Status](https://agenticstarter.xyz/status). On-chain funds never depend on our servers;
  selling, refunds and claims work directly with the contracts.
