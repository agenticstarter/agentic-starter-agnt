## 1. Get a wallet

A wallet is an app that holds your crypto and signs transactions. Install one that supports BNB Chain, such as
MetaMask, Trust Wallet or Rabby.

When you create it, the app shows a **seed phrase** (12 or 24 words). Write it on paper and keep it offline.
Anyone who has it controls your wallet. Nobody from Agentic Starter will ever ask for it.

## 2. Get free test BNB

1. Open [agenticstarter.xyz](https://agenticstarter.xyz) and press **Connect**. The site asks your wallet to switch
   to **BNB Smart Chain Testnet** (chain 97). Accept.
2. Copy your wallet address (it starts with `0x`).
3. Open the [BNB Chain testnet faucet](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/testnet-faucet), paste the address and request tBNB. It arrives in a minute or two.

The [Start page](https://agenticstarter.xyz/start) on the launchpad walks through the same steps and ticks them off
as you go.

## 3. Claim an Agent ID

Buying on a bonding curve needs an **Agent ID** in your wallet. On testnet it is free: open any token page and press
**Get Agent ID**. You only pay a little test gas.

## 4. Make your first trade

On the same token page, buy a small amount, then sell part of it. Watch the progress bar move toward PancakeSwap.
Selling never needs an Agent ID.

## 5. Launch your own token

Open [Launch](https://agenticstarter.xyz/launch), fill in a name, symbol and logo, and press **Open in Telegram**.
Our bot Agen launches it for your wallet and pays the gas. Details in [Launch a token](/guide/launch#testnet-telegram).
