cannot be traded. Join at [airdrop.agenticstarter.xyz](https://airdrop.agenticstarter.xyz), or open it inside
Telegram from our bot Agen.

## Golden hour

One random hour every day (never next to a quiz) is a **golden hour**: every point you earn in that hour counts double (chat, quiz and
curve trades; daily caps still apply). Agen announces each golden hour in the channel and the group, and the Today
card counts it down.

## Agen Gift Hour

Every day from **14:00 to 15:00 UTC**, buy at least **0.01 BNB** of testnet $AGNT on agenticstarter.xyz. Your first buy
of the hour earns your wallet one **gift box**: it pops up on the site, and when you open it you see how much **real
$AGNT** is inside. Every box holds a different amount.

- One box per wallet a day: the first buy of at least 0.01 BNB in the hour counts. A box you don't open right away waits
  for you on the site.
- Gift Hour $AGNT is paid together with the airdrop, to wallets that finished the required quests (Q1–Q3) and were not
  excluded for abuse.
- Your Gift Hour total shows next to your points on the airdrop page and in Agen's /points.
- After opening, share the picture on X or in the Telegram group.
- After the hour, one of the day's boxes is drawn at random as the **lucky box**: its $AGNT is doubled (x2). Agen
  announces it in the group and tells the winner.
- Ten minutes before the hour ends, Agen posts a reminder in the group, and the app reminds wallets that have no box
  yet.
- **Group goal:** the more wallets get a box in a day, the more every box of that day holds: 100 wallets **+5%**, 250
  **+10%**, 500 **+20%**, added after the hour. The bar on the Gift Hour strip shows how close the next level is, and
  Agen's Gift Hour post in the group updates itself as each level is reached.

## Agen Morning Box

Every day from **08:00 to 10:00 UTC**, open the Agentic Starter **app** with your wallet connected: the Morning Box gives
**20 to 100 airdrop points**, one box a day. After 10:00 UTC it is gone until the next morning. The Morning Box is only in
the [installed app](/guide/install-app), not on the website.

Open it on days in a row for a **streak bonus**: 7 days **+100**, 14 days **+200**, 21 days **+300** and 28 days
**+500** points, then the four weeks start over. Miss a day and the streak starts again from 1.

About **1 in 100** Morning Boxes is a **golden box** with **500 points**. When the Morning Box closes, Agen names all of
the day's golden boxes in one post in the group, and the latest lucky and golden boxes are listed on the airdrop page.

**Welcome back:** if you haven't opened a Morning Box for more than 7 days, your first box after the break counts
**x2**. Agen says hello in a DM.

In the app, **Menu → Settings → Notifications** pings you for the Gift Hour, the Morning Box, the daily quiz (5 minutes
before, once a day) and your own airdrop news (eligible, a friend counted, a streak paid). At most 4 a day and never at
night in your time zone. Presale news always comes through.

## The daily quiz

Three sessions a day in the Telegram group, at **03:00, 14:00 and 19:00 UTC**. Each session has three multiple-choice
questions about Agentic Starter, five minutes each. Answer with the A/B/C/D buttons; your last answer counts. The
first 20 correct answers to each question score 5 points. Questions and answers stay in the group to learn from. Want a
reminder five minutes before? Turn on quiz notifications in the app, or pick the rounds in Agen's `/settings`.

One right answer a day keeps your **quiz streak**: 7, 14, 21 and 28 days in a row pay +50, +100, +300 and +400 points.
Miss a day and it starts again. Agen names you in the round's results and sends you a DM with a picture to share.

## How verification works

1. **Wallet.** Connect and sign a message. It is free and sends no transaction.
2. **X (Q1).** Use the Follow and Quote buttons, then paste the link to your quote post. Most posts are checked
   automatically in seconds; the rest go to a quick review by the team. A link already used by another wallet is
   flagged.
3. **Telegram (Q2).** Tap **Open Telegram**, then **Start** in the airdrop bot. It links your Telegram account to your
   wallet and shows Join buttons for the channel and the group; the card ticks by itself. Leaving either removes the
   points.
4. **Chat (Q3).** Agen counts your messages. Messages under 3 characters, repeats, forwards and messages sent
   within 5 seconds of your last one don't count.
5. **On-chain (Q4–Q6)** are read from BNB Chain automatically. A presale bought with another wallet counts once that
   wallet signs a free message to link it (Q5 card). One presale wallet links to one airdrop wallet for good.

Each X account, Telegram account and presale wallet can be linked to one airdrop wallet only.

## How to earn the most points

1. **Finish Q1–Q3 first.** Until then your points don't count for the pool.
2. **Q4 Launch & Trade:** launch one token, make 5 trades of 0.005 BNB or more, and try to get your token to graduate
   (+300).
3. **Every day:** chat in the group (up to 50), answer the quiz (up to 45) and keep the streak, open the Morning Box in
   the app, and catch the Gift Hour.
4. **Invite friends** who really take part: +100 each, +200 at 5 and +1,000 at 20.
5. **Presale:** from 0.05 BNB, up to 10,000 points, plus 100 for each buyer you bring.

Ask Agen in the group ("how do I earn the most points?") or tap **Earn the most** in the airdrop bot: you get a list of
only what *you* still have open, biggest first. Season 1 points stop at the snapshot (the end of the presale); points
after it count for Season 2.

## Am I ready?

The **Ready to claim?** card lists each requirement with what is still missing and how to fix it, plus an estimated
AGNT range. The range depends on the final pool and on how many wallets qualify, so it is a guide, not a promise.

On testnet, wallets that finished Q2 can get a small top-up of test BNB once (0.005 tBNB), while the faucet has
funds.

## Check any wallet

The **Check** box on the airdrop page shows any wallet's quests, points by source, eligibility, rank and estimated
$AGNT. The public top 100 is on the same page.

## Claims

1. At the snapshot, points are converted to $AGNT and a Merkle tree is published.
2. The airdrop contract opens the season with that root and a claim deadline.
3. Eligible wallets claim on the airdrop page (gas only). Unclaimed tokens return to the community pool.

## Fair play

Spam, farm accounts, wash trading and multi-account clusters are removed before the snapshot. The team looks at
wallets that share a network address or trade the same tokens in the same blocks, and decides by hand; nothing is
removed automatically. Every manual change to points is logged with a reason.

If your wallet was excluded by mistake, press **Appeal** in the Ready card and explain in a few sentences. The team
answers on the same card.
