# $AGNT tokenomics

$AGNT is the platform token of Agentic Starter on BNB Chain (BEP-20). Fixed supply, no mint, no tax, no owner.

**Total supply: 1,000,000,000 AGNT**

| Allocation | Share | Tokens | Where it sits | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Presale | 25% | 250M | Presale contract | 100% at claim; unsold AGNT burned at finalize |
| Community & airdrops | 20% | 200M | Community Safe | Seasonal airdrops and the presale early bonus |
| Liquidity (AGNT/BNB) | 15% | 150M | Liquidity Safe | Added to the pool at listing, LP burned |
| Team | 12% | 120M | Vesting contract | 12-month cliff, then 24 months linear |
| Ecosystem & marketing | 12% | 120M | Vesting contract → Ecosystem Safe | 3-month cliff, then 18 months linear |
| Treasury | 10% | 100M | Vesting contract → Treasury Safe | 12 months linear |
| Listing reserve | 6% | 60M | Liquidity Safe | Up to 50M tops up the pool at listing; the rest stays for later listings |

All Safes are 2-of-2 multisigs. The lock rules and the no-sell promise are on [Listing](/token/listing).

## Where value comes from

Every curve trade pays a 1% fee. [Of that fee](/guide/fees):

- **40%** buys back $AGNT on PancakeSwap and burns it,
- **30%** is paid in BNB to $AGNT [stakers](/token/staking),
- **20%** goes to the token's creator (or back to traders in cashback mode), until graduation,
- **10%** funds the treasury.

## Utility at listing

Everything below works from day one. Nothing here promises a price.

| Use | How |
| --- | --- |
| Share of fees | Stake AGNT and earn BNB from every curve trade ([Staking](/token/staking)) |
| Free launches | Stakers with perks (at least 100,000 AGNT staked for 7 days) pay no launch fee, and half price on the standard token factory |
| Votes | Snapshot votes weighted by AGNT staked for at least 7 days |
| Airdrop boost | Season 2 gives stakers a points multiplier |
| Featured on Explore | Burn AGNT on the Ignite board to show a token in the Featured row (1 hour per 10,000 AGNT, at most 24 hours per burn) |

Anything else is on the roadmap and will be put to a vote first.
