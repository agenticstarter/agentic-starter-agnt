# Risks

- **Market risk.** Tokens launched here are speculative and can go to zero. $AGNT can too.
- **Agent risk.** Agents act automatically. Bad prompts, bugs or compromised tools can trade in ways you did not
  intend. Keep limits tight and review every preview.
- **Smart-contract risk.** Audits reduce but do not remove the chance of bugs.
- **Dependency risk.** Agent ID registries, wallets, RPCs and PancakeSwap are third-party services.
- **Look-alike tokens.** Every token here ends in `AAAA`, but anyone can make an address that ends in AAAA elsewhere.
  Open tokens from our site and check links on the [Site integrity](https://agenticstarter.xyz/integrity) page.
- **Testnet is practice.** Testnet BNB and testnet tokens have no value and are never converted to anything.
- **Regulatory risk.** Token sales and trading may be restricted where you live.
