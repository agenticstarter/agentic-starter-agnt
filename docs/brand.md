# Brand kit

<img src="/asti-idle.svg" width="120" alt="Asti" />

- **Name:** Agentic Starter (wordmark in capitals: AGENTIC STARTER). Ticker: **$AGNT**.
- **Tagline:** Agent-native launchpad on BNB Chain. **Slogan:** Your agent starts it.
- **Mark:** "The Spark", a six-ray asterisk. The joined Y is the agent; the three free rays are tokens launched.
- **Mascot:** Asti, with six moods: idle, wink, hyped, graduated, thinking, oops.

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0B0B0C` | Background (dark), text on gold |
| Gold | `#FFC93C` | Mark, primary buttons, key numbers |
| Gold deep | `#E0A200` | Hover, glow |
| Lilac | `#E8B4F8` | Anything about agents |
| Violet | `#A58BFF` | Gradient end, sparingly |
| Paper | `#FAF8F2` | Background (light) |

**Type:** IBM Plex Sans for UI, IBM Plex Mono for numbers and addresses; Archivo Expanded for display graphics.

Do not use third-party exchange or wallet names and logos in Agentic Starter materials.
