# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately:

- Form: [agenticstarter.xyz/security](https://agenticstarter.xyz/security)
- Email: security@agenticstarter.xyz

Do not open a public issue for a security problem. Give the team reasonable time to fix it before sharing details.
Confirmed reports earn bounty points in the airdrop.

In scope: the contracts in `contracts/`, the SDK and the CLI. Notes for auditors are in
[contracts/SECURITY.md](contracts/SECURITY.md).

Official addresses and links: [agenticstarter.xyz/integrity](https://agenticstarter.xyz/integrity). Nobody from the
team will ever DM you first or ask for a seed phrase or private key.
